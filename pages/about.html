How We Started

From a single sewing machine to a global powerhouse.

2018

The Garage Prototype

Frustrated by generic gear that ripped mid-season, our founders sewed the very first Apex jersey in a Brooklyn garage using military-grade parachute thread and a borrowed sublimation printer.

2020

The NY Lab Opens

We launched our first fully automated manufacturing facility in New York, introducing our proprietary microscopic moisture-wicking weaves to local amateur leagues and collegiate teams.

2022

Going Pro

We signed our first multi-year professional team contract, proving on national television that our gear survives the most brutal impacts, slides, and tackles the sport can offer.

2024

Global Expansion

Expanded fulfillment to 15 countries and successfully launched our 100% recycled eco-performance line, proving that sustainability does not require sacrificing a single ounce of durability.

2025

The Innovation Lab

Unveiled our multi-million dollar R&D facility in Austin, dedicated exclusively to engineering phase-change materials and carbon‑infused performance threads.

2026

Eco‑Dominance

Officially reached our milestone of 100% closed‑loop manufacturing across our entire baseline catalog, setting a new global sustainability standard.

Our Core Values

The uncompromising principles that drive our engineering process.

Unbreakable Quality

We subject every garment to a grueling 50-point physical stress test, including severe abrasion, tensile stretching, and heavy sweat corrosion simulations.

Athletic Grit

Our gear is designed specifically for the 4th quarter - when the elements are harsh, muscles are fatigued, and lightweight endurance means the difference between winning and losing.

Relentless Innovation

We are pioneering the use of phase-change temperature-regulating materials and carbon-infused threading to bring aerospace technology into amateur athletics.

Total Transparency

Community Driven
